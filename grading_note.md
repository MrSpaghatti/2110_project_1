Please read the following carefully. Group project grades will be based on each student's individual contribution, not simply on the final group submission.

Although you are working as a group, all group members will not automatically receive the same grade. Each member will be evaluated separately based on the quality and amount of work they contributed to the project.

For each milestone, your report must clearly explain:

    Which part(s) of the project you worked on

    What specific tasks or functionality you implemented

    Any other significant contributions you made to the milestone

There will be some points based on the overall completion and correctness of the project, including whether all required parts are implemented and work together correctly. However, the majority of the evaluation of your work will consider your individual contribution.

If one student completes significantly less work than the other members, that student should not expect to receive the same grade as the members who contributed substantially more. Similarly, completing only a small portion of the project while relying on other group members to complete the remaining work will be reflected in your individual grade.
Important

Do not wait until the end of the project to document your contribution. Keep track of the work you complete throughout each milestone so that you can accurately describe your contributions in the report.

Your group is responsible for working together and ensuring that all required parts of the project are completed, but each student is responsible for making a meaningful and sufficient contribution to the project.

Work as a team, but earn your grade through your own contribution.
